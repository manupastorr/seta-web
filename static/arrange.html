How Arrange works

ListenClick any waveform (or the set waveform on top) to play from there.
Mix pointDrag a track left / right to change where it mixes into the one before. It snaps to phrases that line up (yellow lines); hold Shift for single bars.
OrderDrag a track's number badge to reorder.
TrimDrag a track's left / right edge. It snaps to phrases; Shift: bars.
EQH M L on a track: click to kill / restore a band, scroll for ±3 dB. M in the title mutes.
ExportExport XML saves the order plus memory cues at each mix (Seta mix in, next in, bass swap, cut); your own cues are kept. It always goes to ~/Music/rekordbox/seta.xml, replacing the last export (earlier ones stay in seta-exports/), and each export first backs up your rekordbox library into Seta's rb_backups/. Once in rekordbox: Preferences → Advanced → Database → rekordbox xml → pick that file. After each export, click ↻ on "rekordbox xml" in the tree, open the playlist, select its tracks → right-click → Import To Collection (say yes to overwrite): that brings the cues. Dragging the playlist only brings the order.
WarningsOn a mix label: ⚠ short (under 32 bars for similar tracks, 16 otherwise), ⚠ vocals (two voices audible at once; blue strips on the waveforms show rekordbox's vocal detection), ⚠ key (keys clash on the Camelot wheel), ⚠ energy (drop of 2+ stars), ⚠ % (tempo stretched over 5%). Hover a label for details.
MixEvery mix blends by EQ, with a bass swap where the new track has bass. The red line is where the old track is cut.
CurvesClick a mix label (⇄ 16 bars) to zoom in. Vol, L, M, H lines for both tracks sit on their waveforms. Pick a line with the Out (old track) or In (new track) buttons on the mix label, or click it; drag its points (snap to bars; Shift: free), drag the hollow square at a line's start or end to change it there, double-click it to add a point, Alt+click or Delete removes one, drag the small dot between two points to bend. ↺ recipe goes back to the default.
